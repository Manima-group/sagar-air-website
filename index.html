    About us

    Bringing cutting-edge technology to HVAC & R since 1987

    Sagar Air Pvt Ltd was founded in 1987 by V V Murthy. Since its inception, Sagar Air has strived to bring cutting-edge technology to various sectors of HVAC & R, and is one of the oldest manufacturers of HVAC equipment in Hyderabad.

    Our portfolio covers AHUs, Air Washers, Wet Scrubbers, Air Ventilation Units, FCUs, Plenums, kitchen hoods, industrial Centrifugal and Axial Flow Fans, Air Distribution Products (grilles, dampers, diffusers) and Desiccant Dehumidifiers. We believe in precision and performance: quality standards met with optimum performance at a nominal price.
